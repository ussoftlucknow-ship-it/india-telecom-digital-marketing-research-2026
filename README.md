## 📊 Repository Scope & Telemetry Datasets

This repository provides primary, empirical datasets and analytical benchmarks examining India's digital transformation, high-density bandwidth migration, digital advertising expenditure shifts, and regional marketing employment trends across Uttar Pradesh (UP East) and Northern India.

### Cluster Breakdown:
1. **Cluster A: Telecom Infrastructure & High-Density Bandwidth (8 Studies)**
   - India Internet User Base & Active vs Gross Subscriber Ratios
   - UP East Telecom Circle Rural Penetration & Density Deficit
   - Mobile Data Pricing Index (₹7.13/GB) & Usage Patterns
   - 5G vs 4G Traffic Share Migration (44.36% Bandwidth Inflection)
   - 5G Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) Deployments in Tier-2/3 Uttar Pradesh
   - Tele-density Disparities (UP East 68.4% vs Delhi NCR 271.8%)
   - Mobile Number Portability (MNP) Churn Dynamics

2. **Cluster B: Digital Advertising Expenditure & Industry Spend (8 Studies)**
   - India ₹49,883 Crore Digital Advertising Market Trajectory
   - Shift from Legacy Print/Television to Programmatic Digital (2016–2025)
   - Social Media vs Paid Search vs Online Video Budget Allocations
   - Performance Marketing ROI Benchmarks & Forecast Through 2027
   - Regional FM Radio vs Hyperlocal Digital Marketing Ad Spend in Tier-2 Hubs

3. **Cluster C: Digital Marketing Employment, Salaries & Skills Gap (12 Studies)**
   - Lucknow Regional Digital Marketing Course Fee & ROI Audit
   - Top Training Institutes & Curricula Quality Matrix (Lucknow & UP East)
   - Digital Marketing Executive Hiring Trends & Salary Bands (Naukri JobSpeak)
   - Youth Unemployment & Transition to High-Skill Digital Performance Roles
   - World Economic Forum (WEF) Future of Jobs: AI & Data Marketing Alignment

4. **Cluster D: Regional Business Digitization & MSME Adoption (6 Studies)**
   - Uttar Pradesh MSME Digital Adoption Playbook
   - Lucknow Local Business Digital Lead Generation Models
   - Rural & Peri-Urban Digital Consumer Acquisition Strategies in UP East
   - TRAI & MoSPI Open Data Methodologies for Marketers
